# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page says what information Graph Modeler™ and its website collect, and who sees it. The short version: the app collects nothing, the website does not use
cookies or analytics, and the only personal information that reaches me is what you send me or what is needed to sell you a license.

Graph Modeler is published by Jeffrey J. Long ("I", "me").

## The app

The Graph Modeler app does not collect information about you or your use. It sends no usage statistics, crash reports or other telemetry, and it does not check
for updates. Your projects, the data files you query and the files you publish stay on your computer.

The app starts other programs only on your computer, such as Graphviz to draw a picture. It makes no network connection of its own. When you press a help or
home link, it opens the page in your web browser, and your browser then connects to this website like any other link.

The **Copy** button on the [About tab](/docs/about/) puts your support information (the version, your operating system and a few facts about your
computer, and nothing about your data) on your clipboard. It goes nowhere unless you paste it into a message and send it to me.

## This website

This website is a set of ordinary pages. It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics, and it does not follow you from page to page. Your browser may
remember on your own device whether you chose the light or the dark theme, and I do not receive it.

The site and the downloads are hosted by GitHub. As with any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and the details of your request when you load a page
or download an installer, and it keeps its own logs, which I do not control. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## When you write to me

If you write to [support@graphmodeler.com](mailto:support@graphmodeler.com) or [sales@graphmodeler.com](mailto:sales@graphmodeler.com), I receive your email address,
your name if you give it, and what you send. I use it to answer you, and I keep the messages for as long as they are useful for support or for the record of a purchase.
I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not sell or share your details. Email is handled by Microsoft 365.

Please do not send private or confidential data files. If a project file would help me find a problem, take out anything you do not want to share first.

## When you buy a license

Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects what a purchase needs, such as your name, email address, billing
country or address, and payment details, and its own [privacy policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy) applies to it. I do not see or keep your card number.
I receive the details of the order: your name, your email address, the country, what you bought, and when, so that I can send your license, answer
questions and keep the records that the law and taxes require.

## Who else sees it

I do not sell your information or use it for advertising. It is shared only with the services described above, which do the work of hosting, mail and payment,
and where the law requires it.

## How long I keep it

Email and order records are kept for as long as needed for support, and for the years that tax and accounting rules require records of a sale to be kept.
After that they are deleted.

## Your choices

You may ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or ask me to stop using it, by writing to
[support@graphmodeler.com](mailto:support@graphmodeler.com). I will answer within a reasonable time. Some order records have to be kept, and I will say so if
that stops me from deleting something. Lemon Squeezy has its own way of handling requests about what it holds.

## Children

Graph Modeler is not meant for children, and I do not knowingly collect information about them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version applies from then on.
