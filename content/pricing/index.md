## Questions

**Do I have to pay to try it?** No. Download it, use it, and decide later. Nothing is held back until you buy. The [Download page](/download/) has
the installers.

**Does the license end?** No. You pay once, and the key has no end date.

**How do I use my key?** Paste it into the **About** tab of the program. After you buy, Polar emails you an **Access purchase** link, and it sends you a 6-digit
code and then shows the key. See [The About tab](/docs/about/#your-license).

**Does the key lock the program?** No. Nothing is switched off with or without a key, and if the program cannot reach the license server it carries on.

**Is tax added?** Where the law requires it. [Polar](https://polar.sh), which sells the licenses as the merchant of record, adds sales tax or VAT
at checkout and sends your receipt.

**Can I get an invoice with our company name?** Yes. Tick **I'm purchasing as a business** at checkout to add the business name and a tax ID.

**What is on my card statement?** The charge appears as `POLAR*graph-modeler`.

**Where do I find out what counts as commercial?** On the [License page](/license/), which also has the full license text. If you are not sure,
write to [sales@graphmodeler.com](mailto:sales@graphmodeler.com).

**What if I am not happy with it?** Write to [sales@graphmodeler.com](mailto:sales@graphmodeler.com) within 30 days for your money back. See the
[Refund Policy](/refunds/).
