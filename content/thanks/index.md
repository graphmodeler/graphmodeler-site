# Thank you

Thank you for buying a commercial license for Graph Modeler™. Your purchase is complete.

## Your license key

Polar, which handles the payment, emails you at the address you gave at checkout. The email has your receipt and an **Access purchase** button. Click it, and Polar
sends a 6-digit code to the same address. Type the code on the page that opens, and Polar shows your **license key** and lets you copy it. If the email has not
arrived in a few minutes, look in your junk folder. You can sign in the same way at any time to see your key again.

- Keep the key safe. It belongs to the person or the organization named on the order.
- You can use the key on your own computers. If you reinstall or replace one, enter the same key there.
- To use the key, open Graph Modeler and choose **Enter license key** on the [About tab](/docs/about/#your-license), or in the question the program asks when it
  first starts. Paste the key and press **Check the key**. The About tab then says **Licensed for commercial use** and shows the name on the order. The key is
  checked with Polar when you enter it and about once a month after that; see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
- On your card or bank statement the charge appears as `POLAR*graph-modeler`, because Polar takes the payment. The receipt shows the same name.

## Get the software

The installers for Windows, macOS and Linux are on the [Download page](/download/). Graph Modeler needs [Graphviz](/download/#install-graphviz) to draw
pictures, and works without it for everything else.

## If something is wrong

- **No email, or the wrong email address:** write to [support@graphmodeler.com](mailto:support@graphmodeler.com) with the name on the order.
- **A question about your license:** write to [sales@graphmodeler.com](mailto:sales@graphmodeler.com).
- **You changed your mind:** a full refund within 30 days, no questions asked. See the [Refund Policy](/refunds/).

The [Terms of Service](/terms/) and the [license](/license/) say what the license covers.
