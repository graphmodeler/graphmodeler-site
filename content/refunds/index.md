# Refund Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Graph Modeler™ is free for personal use, so you can try it before you buy a commercial license. If you buy one and it is not right for you, you can have your money back.

## 30 days, no questions asked

You may ask for a full refund within **30 days** of your purchase. I will not ask you why. Write to [sales@graphmodeler.com](mailto:sales@graphmodeler.com) and give
the email address you used for the order and the order number from your receipt.

## What you get back

- The full price you paid, including any tax that was added at checkout.
- It goes back to the payment method you used. Polar, which sells the licenses as the merchant of record, makes the refund. Your bank may take several
  business days to show it.
- Polar may also refund a purchase on its own within 60 days of the sale in some cases, for example to settle a complaint before it becomes a dispute.

## After a refund

A refunded license ends, and you must stop using the software commercially, which means you need a commercial license again for that use. The software stays free for
personal and other non-commercial use, as the [license](/license/) says.

## After 30 days

A request after 30 days is not covered by this policy, but write anyway. I look at each request on its own, especially when the software did not do what this site
said it would. I will tell you what I can do.

## Before you dispute a charge

Please write to me first. I can usually settle a refund faster than a dispute with your bank can, and a dispute can cost both of us more.

## Problems with the software

If the software does not work for you, write to [support@graphmodeler.com](mailto:support@graphmodeler.com) and say what happened. Many problems have a fix that
takes a few minutes, and the [Support](/support/) page lists the common ones. You do not have to be refused a fix to ask for a refund.

## Contact

[sales@graphmodeler.com](mailto:sales@graphmodeler.com) for refunds and licenses; [support@graphmodeler.com](mailto:support@graphmodeler.com) for help with the software.
